# Purrsonal Portfolio Path

I want to build a portfolio that links to my github, linkedin and a page where there is a blog with pictures and short descriptions of the projects i have built, as well as an about me section with a picture of me and my cats. ☺️

I am attaching a generated picture with how I want the layout to look. The book in the middle will act as a button where user can pick if they would like to see github, linkedin, about me section or my project blog. I also want the cats on the sides act as guides and have two different personalities. The white one on the left has a grumpy personality and the brown one on the right is carefree and happy. They should show little quotes matching their personality.
